About

Simple is a discipline.

Saral means simple. The studio exists to turn complicated work into clear, durable systems.

The studio

Built in Sydney. Shaped around the problem.

Saral Forge combines product thinking, software engineering and design. The work begins with the goal, maps the friction around it, and builds the smallest system that creates a meaningful change.

No invented outcomes. No technology theatre. No handover mystery.

Working principles

  1. 01

    Make the problem legible.

    A clear problem saves more time than a clever implementation.

  2. 02

    Design the whole path.

    The useful experience includes the handoffs, failure states and follow-up.

  3. 03

    Leave it easier to change.

    Content, rules and data should outlast any one framework.

Simple software, shaped around real work.
